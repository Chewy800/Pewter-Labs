# Pewter Data for Claude

Pewter Data helps companies explore whether their operating records may be suitable for a licensing opportunity and helps buyers describe the data they need. This plugin guides either person through a short set of questions, summarizes the inquiry, and points them to the relevant Pewter website form.

The plugin does not connect to a server, submit an inquiry, upload company records, inspect private inventory, quote a price, or make a licensing offer. The person chooses whether to leave Claude and submit the form. If they do, the information entered in that form is sent to Pewter through its website. The plugin itself sends no chat content or files to Pewter.

For company owners, use the owner form at https://pewterdata.com/#offer. For buyers, use the data request form at https://pewterdata.com/labs#request. Do not paste raw records, personal data, credentials, client files, patient information, or confidential contracts into the chat.
